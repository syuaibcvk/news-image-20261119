One picture of one day: a newspaper front page for Thursday 19 November
2026. The front page leads with the first-ever trilateral meeting of Trump,
Xi and Putin, held in Shenzhen during the APEC summit. Under it, the Gulf
war widens — Iran fires drones at a US base in Kuwait after American jets
hit a missile site on Qeshm Island. The middle of the page carries three
more stories of the day: GTA VI launches around the world and Vice City
returns after thirteen years; the World Court in The Hague hands down its
historic verdict on whether Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya;
and Cyclone Akvan closes on India's coast between Machilipatnam and Puri.
Along the foot, in brief: a Van Gogh sells for a record $190.4 million,
Anthropic is poised for a $1.4 trillion stock-market debut, Nvidia stops
shipping H-series chips to China, and the Fed's minutes point to further
rate rises.

`main.py` copies the picture to `/output/entry.png` and checks the copy
byte for byte. No network, no clock, no randomness — run it twice and the
two outputs are identical.

## Try it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
sha256sum out/entry.png entry.png
```
